The Purpose

Rather than being a library of content and courses you'll never complete, this is an actively engaged network of creators and guides. With regular workshops, writing challenges, and small accountability groups, the Alliance is here to keep you on your path to freedom.

  • 01

    Live calls and workshops

    Bi-weekly conversations with Jesse James Carver and guest speakers on what's actually working in the independent creator economy. Ask your questions, get your answers.

  • 02

    The recordings vault

    Every session from the 2026 Future-Proof Creator Summit, an event that cost $497 to attend in person. Plus every past call, workshop, and podcast from the community. All of it becomes available the moment you join.

  • 03

    The 90-Day Creator Sprint

    July through September. This is already underway, but every workshop so far is recorded, the upcoming ones are on the calendar, and the sprinter chat is open. See the note below on joining mid-sprint.

  • 04

    Direct access

    To Jesse, to speakers, to levels of collaboration with fellow seekers that will accelerate your development. These are levels of conversation that just don't happen on the public platforms.

Joining the Sprint mid‑stream

The Sprint is a post-summit bonus that runs in July, August, and September, so part of it has already happened. Every workshop is recorded and every conversation is still there, so you can work backward through what you missed and take what's useful at your own pace.

Accountability sub-groups formed together on July 1, which means you won't be placed in one automatically. The accountability channel is where groups post when they're taking new people, so with a little bit of effort you will find your crew.

Most of the sprinters came from the in‑person Creator Summit that we hosted in Canada in June. If you're thinking about attending the 2027 Summit, this is the shortest path to already knowing the people who will be there.
