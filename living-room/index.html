The Vision

Step out of performance mode for an afternoon

The Living Room is a half-day gathering for entrepreneurs — founders, coaches, creators, and business owners who spend most of their week explaining themselves to a client, a team, or an audience. You'll know who's in the room before anything physical starts, then move through breathwork, sauna, and cold plunge with people you've actually met.

You don't get much unscripted time in this life. This is a few hours to think out loud with people who understand the pressure of running a business, and come back with a clearer read on what you're doing and why.

What to Expect

Real introductions first, then the physical work

The afternoon opens with guided introductions: a short, structured round where everyone says plainly what they do, what they're building, and what they could use help with. That happens before anything physical, so by the time breathwork starts, sauna and cold plunge become free-flow conversation, not small talk with strangers. The day closes with a talk on identity and self-perception, followed by a facilitated closing round that puts what you've learned about the room into practice.

You'll be in an intimate group, capped at 10 people, so the experience stays personal rather than performative.

Why It Matters

Most people leave clearer, not just calmer

Most networking events trade in surface — pitches, introductions, cards you'll never look at again. Most wellness spaces send you home alone with whatever you figured out in silence. Neither gets you all the way there.

The Living Room does both in one afternoon: real introductions with other entrepreneurs, then a real physical reset built on top of that context. You'll leave with more than a nice afternoon behind you — a clearer sense of what you're building, who it's actually for, and the capacity to go deliver on it.
