What we stand for

Five values.

  1. Connection to natureGood work grows out of a life rooted in the living world, away from the screen.
  2. Creative sovereigntyYour voice, your ideas and your body of work belong to you.
  3. Social sovereigntyReal relationships, built on your own terms, that don't depend on any one platform.

Questions

Before you join.

Why Eden? Wasn't this on Circle?

It was. The community moved from Circle to Eden in October 2026, and the Circle community is closing. If you were a member there, join on Eden. If you were at the 2026 summit, claim founding access instead.

Monthly or yearly?

Monthly is $3 (your first month is $1). Yearly is $15, about 58% less over a year. Both get you everything above.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It's a simple subscription with no contract.

Does membership include the summit recordings?

No. The 2027 recordings go to 2027 ticket holders (GA, VIP and Virtual all include them), and the 2026 recordings to 2026 attendees and founding members. Membership gets you 15% off the ticket.

Does a summit ticket include membership?

No, they're separate. Plenty of people join first for the 15% off, then buy their ticket.
