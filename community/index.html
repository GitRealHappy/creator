What we stand for

Seek Your Treasure

Years spent building someone else's dream, on work that means nothing to you, slowly breed nihilism. The Alliance is for people willing to do the inner work: to excavate what's really in them, then build wealth by putting it into the world.

  1. Your true potentialWe question the values we were conditioned into and keep reaching for who we actually are, even when the default path looks safer.
  2. Creative sovereigntyYour voice, your ideas and your body of work belong to you.
  3. Economic sovereigntyA living earned from your own vision, so your best hours go into a dream you chose.
  4. Social sovereigntyReal relationships, built on your own terms, that don't depend on any one platform.
  5. Connection to natureGood work grows out of a life rooted in the living world, away from the screen.

Questions

Before you join.

Why Eden? Wasn't this on Circle?

It was. The community moved from Circle to Eden in October 2026, and the Circle community is closing. If you were a member there, join on Eden. If you were at the 2026 summit, claim founding access instead.

Monthly or yearly?

Monthly is $3 (your first month is $1). Yearly is $15, about 58% less over a year. Both get you everything above.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It's a simple subscription with no contract.

Does membership include the summit recordings?

Not by itself. Every 2027 ticket (GA, VIP and Virtual) includes both the 2027 recordings and the 2026 recordings, along with a year of membership to watch them in. 2026 attendees and founding members get the 2026 recordings too. Membership on its own gets you 10% off the ticket.

Does a summit ticket include membership?

Yes. Every summit ticket (GA, VIP, Virtual, and each seat in a 3-Pack) includes a year of membership. It starts when you buy your ticket and runs until a year after the summit, June 2028. After that it ends unless you renew at $3/month or $15/year. You can also join first for the 10% off, then buy your ticket.
