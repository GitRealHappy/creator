    I.

    Room to Think

    On the first evening, we’ll all have the chance to introduce ourselves and voice our intentions for 2027, to whatever depth you like. Then Jesse leads three mastermind sessions. Each turn belongs to one person: you share what you’re building, and the rest of the circle sits with it and offers what they see. Across the three sessions, everyone gets nudged toward the clarity and ideas they came for. Between them is unhurried time: big dinners, the desert, and long conversations with like minds.

    iWhat happens in Sedona

    Business brainstorming with a close circle who can keep it confidential.

    iiYour plan, your way

    Present as much or as little as you like. No homework.

    iiiBlocks of nothing

    Open time on purpose: work on your own projects, walk alone in the desert, sit in the hot tub, or do nothing at all. Every meal is taken care of in the house, so the time goes to each other and to the conversations that happen between sessions.

    II.

    The Adventure

    One afternoon, the table moves to the backcountry: Sedona’s canyons and vortex sites in a private Jeep expedition, with a guide who knows the land.

    The desert at dawn

    A slow walk in the desert each morning, with a notebook if you like, before the day gets going.

    The dark sky

    Sedona protects its night sky. On a clear January night, the stars come right down to the rocks.

    The weather

    January days in Sedona are mild and bright; nights drop close to freezing. Fire, sauna and warmth are the point. Bring layers for the morning walks.
