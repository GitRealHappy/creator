A word from Jesse

This gathering is something you help create as much as experience. What you bring is part of what everyone else receives, and when each of us brings our gifts into the room, something emerges that none of us could plan.

To the ones who are crazy enough.

If I sent you this invitation, I see you as someone who is embracing the full power of your mind.

In June this year, a hundred creators gathered in Abbotsford BC for the Future-Proof Creator Summit, and it was a profound experience for me. Something incredibly powerful happened in that room. I’ve seen the effects in people’s lives and in their businesses, and the collaborative energy has been magical to witness. I really believe that time together in person with great minds, fully immersed, amplifies all of our abilities, strengths and creative power.

For ten great creators, plus myself and strategist Nick Serpico also at the table, this is the kind of mastermind that has a history of compounding into great futures.

Room to Think

On the first evening, we’ll all have the chance to introduce ourselves and voice our intentions for 2027, to whatever depth you like. Then Jesse leads three mastermind sessions. Each turn belongs to one person: you share what you’re building, and the rest of the circle sits with it and offers what they see. Across the three sessions, everyone gets nudged toward the clarity and ideas they came for. Between them is unhurried time: big dinners, the desert landscape, and rich conversations with like minds.

What happens in Sedona

Business brainstorming with a close circle.

Your plan, your way

Present as much or as little as you like.

Blocks of nothing

Open time on purpose: work on your own projects, walk alone in the desert, sit in the hot tub, or do nothing at all. Every meal is taken care of in the house, so the time goes to each other and to the conversations that happen between sessions.

Your three days here

ThursdayJan 21

Lunch together in Phoenix for anyone who’d like to join. The vans leave Phoenix at 1:00 pm for the road trip up through the desert, and the house opens to us at 4:00 pm, the rocks toward Mammoth Rock and the Cathedral side filling the glass, with the sun setting around 5:45 pm. Welcome dinner, then a short intro circle by the fireplace: who you are and what you came for.

FridayJan 22

Coffee on the wraparound deck and a slow desert morning. Late morning, the first mastermind session. Lunch, then a long block of nothing: your own projects, a walk alone, the sauna or the hot tub. Late afternoon, the second session. The chef cooks dinner.

SaturdayJan 23

A big breakfast, then the third session, in a room full of light. In the afternoon, the Jeeps take us into the red rocks. Dinner, then the evening around the fire pit.

SundayJan 24

A final breakfast and a closing circle with the morning light on the rocks. Check-out is at 10:00 am, and the drive back down to Phoenix begins around 1:00 pm.

Indicative. Only the travel and check-in times are fixed; the full schedule follows by email.

    Getting there

    Land at Phoenix Sky Harbor by about 11:30 am on Thursday, January 21, and book your flight home for after about 4:00 pm on Sunday. Everyone is welcome at lunch in Phoenix on Thursday (we’ll send the spot by email).
