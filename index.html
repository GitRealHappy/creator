What it is

A conference for wisdom seekers.

The people who come are building the new meaning economy: creators and one-person businesses working out how to earn a living without giving up their sense of deeper purpose.

  • 01

    Talks from people who've done the work

    Live keynotes and panels from creators who built the thing they're talking about.

    The inner transformation work as well as the social media strategy for successful brand construction.

  • 02

    Working sessions

    Workshops you take part in, with your own work on the table, instead of another video to watch.

Before you book

Top questions.

Can I get a refund?

No, all sales are final. Tickets are transferable until May 1, 2027, though: email jesse@thelivinginternet.com with the new attendee's name and email.

What if I can't travel?

Get a Virtual ticket ($147 until January 1$197). Every talk streams live, there's a chatroom for virtual attendees, and the recordings are yours afterward, along with the 2026 summit recordings and a year of Alliance membership.

How do I get to Abbotsford?

Abbotsford is the city in the country, about an hour east of Vancouver. Fly into Abbotsford International (YXX), or Vancouver International (YVR) for more routes. The venue is announced by January 2027. More on getting here.

What's included with each ticket?

General Admission and VIP include the Friday welcome dinner and lunches. VIP adds the Saturday dinner with the speakers, front-row seating and the Sunday After-Party (GA can add the After-Party for $77). All three tickets, Virtual included, come with the 2027 recordings, the full 2026 summit recordings, and a year of Alliance membership.

Can I bring my team?

Yes. The GA 3-Pack is $997$1,297 and the VIP 3-Pack is $3,397$4,000. Send all three names and emails to Jesse by May 1, 2027.

Is there an age limit?

The summit is 19+ unless you're accompanied by a chaperone.
