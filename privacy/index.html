Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and who sees it. Questions? Email Jesse

Effective November 7, 2026

1. Who we are

The Living Internet Alliance ("we", "us") is a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Jesse James Boyes in Abbotsford, British Columbia, Canada. Jesse is responsible for how we handle personal information. You can reach him at jesse@thelivinginternet.com.

We follow Canada's privacy laws, including British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act and, where it applies, the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and email address, and for group or sponsor purchases, the names and emails of the people attending.
  • Purchase details: what you bought, when, the amount, your billing address and country. Card details are collected by Stripe; we only see things like the card type and last four digits.
  • Event details you choose to give us: dietary or accessibility needs, and anything else you share to help us host you well.
  • Community activity: your profile and posts if you join our community on Circle.
  • Messages: emails and messages you send us.
  • Photos and video: taken at our events, as described in our Terms.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver what you bought: tickets, live stream access, memberships and digital products.
  • To send you information about events you've registered for, including schedule changes and access links.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep accounting and tax records, as the law requires.
  • To plan and improve our events.
  • To send newsletters and announcements, only if you've subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with sponsors unless you've agreed to that.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the services that help us run things, and only what they need:

  • Stripe (payments) and Ticket Tailor (ticketing)
  • Circle (our community platform)
  • Substack (our newsletter)
  • StreamYard (live streams)
  • Google Workspace (email and file storage)
  • Event venues and caterers, for guest lists and dietary needs
  • Our accountant, for bookkeeping and tax filing

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Where it's stored

Several of the services above are based in the United States or elsewhere outside Canada. Your information may be stored and processed there and may be accessible to authorities in those countries under their laws.

6. How long we keep it

  • Purchase and accounting records: at least six years, as Canadian tax law requires.
  • Event-specific details such as dietary needs: deleted within 12 months after the event.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
  • Community profiles: until you delete your account or ask us to.

7. This website

This website doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds a Substack signup form; those services may receive your IP address and set cookies under their own privacy policies.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to how we use it (some records, like tax records, we're required to keep). Email jesse@thelivinginternet.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

9. Security

We use reputable services with strong security, keep access to personal information limited to the people who need it, and never store full card numbers.

10. Age

Our events and services are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know by email.

12. Contact

The Living Internet Alliance · Abbotsford, British Columbia, Canada · jesse@thelivinginternet.com

See also our Terms of Service.
