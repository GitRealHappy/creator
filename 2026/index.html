The story

The first summit.

About a hundred creators filled the room, most of them flying in from other countries. In-person tickets sold out.

Many more joined on the live stream. For three days there were keynote talks, workshops, panel discussions, warm meals, and late nights that ran past the last sessions.

Jesse wasn't sure he'd run it again. By day three, seeing what was happening for the people in that room made it non-negotiable. So it's happening again: June 4–6, 2027, back in Abbotsford.
