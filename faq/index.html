Getting here

Where is the summit?

Abbotsford, British Columbia: the city in the country, about an hour east of Vancouver. The exact venue will be announced by January 2027 (subscribers to the Substack hear first).

Which airport should I fly into?

Abbotsford International (YXX) is the closest, right in town. Vancouver International (YVR) has more routes and is about 90 minutes away by car.

How do I get from the airport?

Taxis and rideshare apps run from both airports. Attendees also organize carpools from YVR inside the Alliance; see rideshares and room-shares below.

Where to stay

Is there a hotel block?

No. We'll recommend three hotels close to the venue, with distances, as soon as the venue is named in January. Book whichever suits you.

Rideshares, room-shares and meetups

Can I share a ride or a room with other attendees?

Yes. There's a space for this inside the Alliance, where attendees plan carpools, split rooms and set up meetups before the summit. Join the Alliance to get in.

What's included

What does each ticket include?

General Admission: every talk and working session in person, the Friday welcome dinner, lunches, the recordings from 2027 and 2026, and a year of Alliance membership. The After-Party is a $77 add-on at checkout.

VIP: everything in GA, plus the Saturday dinner with the speakers, reserved front-row seating and the Sunday After-Party. Only 30 seats.

Virtual: every talk streamed live, a chatroom with attendees worldwide, the recordings from 2027 and 2026, and a year of Alliance membership.

Do I get the recordings?

Yes, with every ticket type. You get the 2027 recordings after the event, plus the full 2026 summit recordings right away. They live in a ticket-holder space inside the Alliance, which your ticket's year of membership gives you access to.

What's the schedule?

Friday, June 4: 1:00pm start, welcome dinner at 6:00pm. Saturday, June 5: 9:00am–4:00pm, VIP dinner at 7:00pm. Sunday, June 6: 10:00am–3:00pm, After-Party at 8:00pm.

Policies and prices

Can I get a refund?

No refunds. All sales are final.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Yes, until May 1, 2027. Email jesse@thelivinginternet.com with the new attendee's name and email.

Is there an age limit?

The summit is 19+ unless you're accompanied by a chaperone.

When do prices go up?

Early-bird prices (GA $397, VIP $1,297, Virtual $147) end when 2027 begins. On January 1, they go to $497, $1,497 and $197.

Early-bird ended on January 1. Prices are now GA $497, VIP $1,497 and Virtual $197.

Will there be a Black Friday or holiday sale?

No. The early-bird price is the lowest public price. Alliance members will likely get windows in 2027 when early-bird prices return.

Is there a discount for members?

Yes. Alliance members get 10% off any ticket, on top of the current price, with a code shared inside the community. Membership is $3/month or $15/year. Ticket holders get a year of membership included, so they don't need to join first. See the Alliance.
